Tech B.Tech, which stands for Bachelor of Technology, is an undergraduate degree program awarded in the field of engineering and technology. The content covered in a B.Tech program can vary depending on the specialization or branch of engineering chosen by the student. However, there are some core subjects and common topics that are typically included in most B.Tech programs. Here is an overview of the content covered in a typical B.Tech curriculum: SR NO. Department Available Seats Duration 1 Computer Science Engineering 60 4 Year 2 Information Technology 30 4 Year 3 Electrical Engineering 60 4 Year 4 Mechanical Engineering 60 4 Year 5 Civil Engineering 60 4 Year 6 Electronics & Communication Engineering 60 4 Year 6 Agricultural Engineering 30 4 Year Mathematics: B.Tech programs usually include several courses in mathematics, including calculus, differential equations, linear algebra, probability and statistics, numerical methods, and discrete mathematics. These courses provide a strong mathematical foundation for engineering principles and applications. Physics: Basic principles of physics such as mechanics, thermodynamics, optics, electromagnetism, and quantum physics are taught to provide a fundamental understanding of physical phenomena and their applications in engineering. Chemistry: Fundamental concepts of chemistry, including atomic structure, chemical bonding, stoichiometry, and chemical reactions, are covered to understand the chemical aspects related to engineering materials and processes. Computer Science: Introduction to computer programming, data structures, algorithms, and computer organization are usually part of the B.Tech curriculum. These courses equip students with programming skills and an understanding of computer systems. Engineering Mechanics: This subject covers the principles of statics and dynamics, including forces, equilibrium, motion, and the behavior of solids and fluids. It forms the basis for understanding the mechanical behavior of structures and machines. Electrical and Electronics Engineering: This branch covers topics such as circuit analysis, electronic devices and circuits, digital electronics, signals and systems, power systems, and electrical machines. It provides a foundation for understanding electrical and electronic systems. Mechanical Engineering: Subjects in mechanical engineering include thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, solid mechanics, manufacturing processes, machine design, and robotics. These topics focus on understanding the principles and applications of mechanical systems. Civil Engineering: Civil engineering subjects cover areas such as structural analysis and design, geotechnical engineering, transportation engineering, environmental engineering, and construction management. It deals with the design and construction of infrastructure and buildings. Other Specializations: B.Tech programs also offer specialization options such as computer science and engineering, information technology, electronics and communication engineering, aerospace engineering, biotechnology, and more. Each specialization has its own set of core subjects and elective courses. Admission 2023 AKTU: Minor Degree Course for B.Tech (As per New Education Policy) First year students taking admission in BTech from the new academic session will be able to study new subjects along with their original subjects. Students will get the opportunity to study Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Data Science, Waste Technology, Robotics and other additional subjects along with the basic branch of BTech to diversify the new knowledge and courses. For this study, students will be given a minor degree along with a basic degree. The new session 2022-23 of Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Provisional University (AKTU) will see major changes in the B.Tech syllabus. For the first time, under the National Education Policy 2020, the option of granting a minor degree in another subject has been given along with the basic degree of B. The subjects of minor degree have been fixed for the students taking admission in B.Tech.
